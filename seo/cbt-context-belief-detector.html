Why Context Matters

A thought like "I'm not good enough" is ambiguous on its own. But add context and it becomes specific:

  • At work → "I'm incompetent" (competence belief)
  • In a relationship → "I'm unlovable" (social-worth belief)
  • With family → "I'm a burden" (belonging belief)

A classifier that ignores context will misclassify thoughts. This tool uses a domain-keyed lookup table 鈥?the rule-based equivalent of an attention mechanism. No AI, no NLP, no transformer required.
