3 Cognitive Distortions for Emotional Drain

🦸 Superhero Syndrome

"I should be able to handle everything", "If I don't do it, no one will", "Asking for help is weakness"

Reframe: "I am human with finite energy. Sustainable giving requires receiving. My worth is not measured by my capacity to endure. Asking for help is strategic, not weak. I model healthy boundaries for those I care for."

⏰ Productivity = Worth

"Rest is lazy", "I should be doing more", "If I slow down, everything will fall apart"

Reframe: Rest is productive. Recovery is maintenance. My worth exists independently of my output. The world will not end if I take a 20-minute break. Sustainable pacing prevents catastrophic burnout.

💔 Emotional Contagion Trap

"Their pain is my pain", "I must fix their feelings", "If I set boundaries, they will abandon me"

Reframe: Empathy without boundaries becomes enmeshment. I can hold space without absorbing. Others are responsible for their emotional regulation. Healthy boundaries attract healthy relationships.
