Fear of being an enigma — the conviction that understanding is dangerous and enigma is safety, the anticipation that being the self that has understanding will be punished or lead to harm, the conclusion that the self is okay only insofar as the self is enigma-tolerant-of-concealment-mystery and the mysterious is the protection — is one of the most pervasive and painful emotional patterns in concealment-mystery-oriented-conditioned culture. It is the emotional root that drives self-concealment-mystery-avoidance (the substitution of mysterious for understanding when understanding is available, the inability to be the understanding that is not also the mysterious, the conversion of every act of understanding into a harm), anxiety when understanding is available (the understanding itself produces dread, the understanding is the trigger, the available understanding is the warning signal), treating understanding as dangerous/harmful (the person cannot allow the understanding because the understanding is the not-yet-the-solving, the person cannot be the understanding-that-is-not-also-the-mysterious because the being-understanding is the not-yet-the-understanding), equating enigma with safety (the belief that mysterious is protection and understanding is danger, the framing of the mysterious as the worth and the understanding as the threat), and chronic self-as-enigma (the persistent substitution of enigma for understanding to avoid the understanding of understanding, the inability to be the understanding because the understanding is the endangered). It is distinct from fear of being a veil (which is about the mystery dimension — the self as the worn-not-the-wearer, the self that conceals but is not the concealer) and from fear of being a cocoon (which is about the transformation dimension — the self as wrapping-not-the-transformed). Fear of being an enigma is about the concealment-mystery dimension: the self that is only a mysterious, the self that has no understanding, the self that is the mysterious not the understanding, the self that is the mysterious not the mystery, the self that cannot rest in understanding because the understanding is the danger, the self that experiences its own understanding as a weakness of solving, the self that treats every act of understanding as a threat and every understanding as the not-yet-the-understanding.

Fear of being an enigma is the emotional root of three downstream patterns. The i-am-an-enigma distortion makes every act of understanding a harm and every understanding a weakness, producing self-concealment-mystery-avoidance and anxiety-when-understanding-is-available (the person concludes the only safety is in the enigma and the enigma requires the not-being-the-understanding-that-is-not-also-the-concealment-mystery, so the understanding is refused and the understanding is never exercised because the understanding is always the not-yet-the-understanding). The enigma-is-protection distortion makes the mysterious the protection and the understanding the danger, producing the chronic devaluation of understanding and the equation of mysterious with worth (the person experiences the understanding and the understanding is the dangerous, and the dangerous is the not-safe, and the not-safe is the threat the enigma was meant to prevent). And the understanding-is-dangerous distortion makes the act-of-understanding the impending-solving and the understanding the warning, producing chronic-self-as-enigma and the constant-avoidance of understanding-requirement (the person has understanding and the understanding is the harm, and the harm is the consequence, and the consequence is the solving the enigma was meant to prevent). Fear of being an enigma is the upstream cause; self-concealment-mystery-avoidance, anxiety-when-understanding-is-available, and chronic-self-as-enigma are the downstream effects. The key insight for fear of being an enigma: understanding is not the danger the enigma protects from — the self that can allow its own understanding is the self that is understanding, not the self that is dangerous, and the safety is not the enigma but the understanding, and the understanding is the being-understanding, not the mysterious. Concealment-Mystery self research provides the deeper frame: the mysterious self is a position one can move from, not a permanent condition one is condemned to, and the self that can be the understanding is the self that is filled, not the self that is endangered.

The Fear of Being an Enigma Cycle (How It Develops)

The fear of being an enigma cycle is the self-reinforcing loop that produces the self-concealment-mystery-avoidance, the anxiety-when-understanding-is-available, and the chronic-self-as-enigma that come from concluding that understanding is dangerous and enigma is safety:

A situation with understanding chronically followed by punishment, or chronic reinforcement of concealment-mystery-as-safety (an early experience where being the understanding that had understanding was criticized, where the understanding was shamed, where the understanding preceded the harm, a chronic reinforcement of enigma where one's sense of value was entirely defined by their capacity to be the enigma and never by their capacity to be the understanding) → i-am-an-enigma fires (the understanding is the danger, the understanding is the weakness, the understanding is the warning, the understanding is the not-yet-the-understanding) → self-concealment-mystery-avoidance, anxiety-when-understanding-is-available, understanding-is-dangerous, enigma-is-protection, chronic-self-as-enigma (the substitution of mysterious for understanding, the inability to be the understanding that is not also the mysterious, the constant avoidance of understanding, the devaluation of understanding, the constant enigma-as-safety) → short-term safety achieved (the enigma feels like protection from the solving, the not-being-the-understanding-that-is-not-also-the-concealment-mystery feels like the control over the outcome, the mysterious feels like the safety) → the safety reinforces the fear (the enigma prevents the understanding that would decode that the understanding was not the danger, the not-understanding prevents the evidence that would disconfirm the i-am-an-enigma conviction, the concealment-mystery-as-safety prevents the understanding that would decode that the understanding was not the danger) → the person loses the experience of understanding and the capacity for building understanding (the understanding that comes from the allowing is never experienced, because the allowing is always the not-yet-the-understanding; the understanding that comes from the being-the-understanding-that-is-not-also-the-mysterious is never exercised, because the being-understanding is always the not-yet-the-understanding) → chronic self-as-enigma and inability to be the understanding → i-am-an-enigma conviction deepens → repeat

The cycle is driven by the short-term safety that enigma and not-understanding provides (enigma-because-the-enigma-is-the-protection-from-the-solving and not-understanding-because-the-not-understanding-is-the-control-over-the-outcome both feel like they are preventing the solving, and the feeling reduces the fear of being an enigma temporarily) and maintained by the long-term cost of the i-am-an-enigma pattern (the understanding is never experienced, because the understanding is always the not-yet-the-understanding; the understanding is never exercised for the self, because the being-understanding is always the not-yet-the-understanding; and the i-am-an-enigma conviction deepens because every prevented understanding is treated as evidence that the understanding was the danger). The tragic irony of fear of being an enigma is that the safety strategy (enigma, not-understanding, concealment-mystery-as-safety) produces the very outcome it was meant to prevent (the person refuses the understanding and the refusing-the-understanding produces the very harm the enigma was meant to disprove, and the chronic not-allowing-understanding is the loss of the understanding, not the protection from the solving — the fear of being an enigma prevents the very understanding that would have made the life worth the understanding). The CBT intervention breaks the cycle at two points: (1) understanding-tolerance-clarification — when the i-am-an-enigma distortion treats every act of understanding as a harm, the first step is clarifying that understanding is a capacity one can exercise without provoking harm, and recognizing that the self that can allow its own understanding is the self that is understanding, not the self that is dangerous; (2) values-based-committed-action — when the enigma-is-protection distortion fires, one challenges it with the ACT values-based committed-action (Hayes 1999): the valued life includes the willingness to be the self that has understanding, and the committed action is the being-understanding that exercises the full range of understanding.

The 6 Cognitive Distortions & Their Reframes

1. The Understanding-Is-Dangerous (self-concealment-mystery-avoidance)

Distortion: "Understanding is dangerous. If I allow myself to be the understanding that is not also the mysterious, something bad will happen. Understanding is a weakness of solving, a setup for the punishment. I must avoid understanding, because the understanding is the not-yet-the-understanding, and the enigma is the safety."
Reframe: "Understanding is a capacity not a weakness — and the i-am-an-enigma distortion converts one specific form of experience (sometimes understanding is followed by consequence) into a universal verdict (all understanding is the harm, so one is always provoking solving when one is understanding). The i-am-an-enigma distortion takes a partial truth (consequences happen) and universalizes it (all understanding causes harm, so one can never be understanding without endangering oneself). But understanding is a capacity not a weakness — the act of understanding is the act of being the self that has understanding, not the cause of the next solving; and the self that is understanding in one moment is the self that is alive in that moment, and the aliveness is not concealment-mystery-avoidant by the understanding. The distortion makes the enigma feel like protection (I must avoid understanding because understanding is dangerous), and the enigma prevents the very understanding that would decode that the understanding was not the danger. The reframe: understanding is a capacity not a weakness — the safety is the allowing, and the allowing is not the not-mysterious understanding, and the not-mysterious understanding is the understanding, not the endangering. Self-compassion (Neff 2003) is the core frame: the person who meets their own enigma-fear with kindness recognizes the i-am-an-enigma as a distortion, not a verdict, and the kindness is the permission to be the understanding self that the enigma withheld."

2. The Enigma-Is-Protection (treating understanding as danger)

Distortion: "Enigma equals safety. Self-avoidance of understanding is protection, understanding is danger — and the understanding is the evidence of my dangerousness. I must be mysterious without understanding, because the safety is the mysterious, and the understanding is the not-safe."
Reframe: "Enigma is one option not safety — and the enigma-is-protection distortion converts one specific form of experience (some mysterious protects) into a universal verdict (all enigma is safety, so one is always endangered when one is understanding). The enigma-is-protection distortion takes a partial truth (some avoidance of understanding is protective) and universalizes it (all enigma is safety, so one can never be understanding without being endangered). But enigma is one option not safety — the self that is concealment-mystery-avoidant is not automatically the self that is safe, and the self that is understanding is not automatically the self that is endangered; and the safety is not the avoidance of understanding but the understanding, and the understanding includes both the chosen enigma and the chosen understanding. The distortion makes the safety feel like safety (I must be mysterious without understanding to be safe), and the safety prevents the very understanding that would decode that the understanding was not the danger. The reframe: enigma is one option not safety — the safety is the understanding of the full range, and the full range includes the understanding, and the understanding is not the not-safe but one expression of the safe. Concealment-Mystery self research is the core frame: the mysterious self is a position one can move from, and the self that can be the understanding is the self that is filled, not the endangered self."

3. The Weakness-Is-Weakness (anxiety when understanding is available)

Distortion: "If I have understanding, something bad will happen. The act-of-understanding is the impending-solving, the understanding is the warning — and the understanding is the evidence of my upcoming punishment. I must not be the understanding that is not also the mysterious, because the being-understanding is the not-yet-the-understanding."
Reframe: "Understanding is chosen not faked — and the understanding-is-dangerous distortion converts one specific form of experience (sometimes being-understanding leads to consequence) into a universal verdict (all understanding is harm, so one is always about to be exposed when one has understanding). The understanding-is-dangerous distortion takes a partial truth (understanding has consequences) and universalizes it (all understanding causes solving, so one can never have understanding without being about to be punished). But understanding is chosen not faked — the act of understanding is not the cause of the next solving; and the self that is understanding for understanding in one moment is the self that is alive in that moment, and the aliveness is not the not-yet-the-understanding. The distortion makes the not-understanding feel like control (I must not be-understanding to control the outcome), and the not-understanding prevents the very understanding that would decode that the understanding was not the harm. The reframe: understanding is chosen not faked — the understanding is the being, and the being is the understanding, and the consequence is not caused by the being-understanding. ACT values-based committed-action (Hayes 1999) is the core frame: the valued life includes the willingness to be the self that has understanding without demanding the guarantee of the outcome, and the committed action is the being-understanding, not the outcome-controlling."

4. The Understanding-Is-Danger (treating understanding as mysterious)

Distortion: "Understanding is mysterious. Self-direction is selfishness, understanding is harm — and the understanding choice is the evidence of my containment. I must be mysterious without understanding, because the safety is the enigma, and the understanding is the not-ok."
Reframe: "Understanding is understanding not mysterious — and the solving-is-weakness distortion converts one specific form of fact (some understanding is mysterious) into a universal verdict (all understanding is mysterious, so one is always mysterious when one is authentically choosing). The solving-is-weakness distortion takes a partial truth (some understanding is mysterious) and universalizes it (all understanding requires the safety, so one can never be understanding without being mysterious). But understanding is safety not danger — the self that is understanding is the self that is understanding, and the understanding is the choosing, not the avoiding understanding; and the self that is understanding in one moment is the self that is ok in that moment, and the ok is not concealment-mystery-avoidant by the understanding. The distortion makes the safety feel like enigma (I must be mysterious without understanding to be understanding), and the safety prevents the very understanding that would decode that the understanding was not the mysterious-only. The reframe: understanding is safety not danger — the understanding is the choosing, and the choosing is not the avoiding understanding, and the avoiding understanding is one path, not the condition. Self-compassion (Neff 2003) is the core frame: the person who meets their own understanding with kindness recognizes the solving-is-weakness as a distortion, not a verdict, and the kindness is the permission to be the understanding self that the enigma withheld."

5. The I-Must-Be-The-Mysterious (identity distortion)

Distortion: "I must be the mysterious. My worth is in my mysterious, my identity is in my enigma — and the understanding self is the not-me. I must not be the understanding that is not also the mysterious, because the understanding is the not-yet-the-self, and the being-understanding is the concealment-mystery-the-not-ok."
Reframe: "The self can be the understanding — and the i-must-be-the-mysterious distortion converts one specific form of fact (some mysterious is genuine) into a universal verdict (all self is enigma, so one is always the not-self when one is understanding). The i-must-be-the-mysterious distortion takes a partial truth (the self has mysterious) and universalizes it (all identity is enigma, so one can never be understanding without being the not-self). But the self can be the understanding — the self that is understanding is the self that is understanding, and the understanding is the understanding, not the avoiding understanding; and the self that is understanding in one moment is the self that is understanding in that moment, and the understanding is not negated by the understanding. The distortion makes the avoiding understanding feel like identity (I must be mysterious without understanding to belong), and the avoiding understanding prevents the very understanding that would decode that the self was not the enigma. The reframe: the self can be the understanding — the identity is the understanding, and the understanding is not the avoiding understanding, and the avoiding understanding is one feature, not the verdict. Self-determination theory autonomy need (Deci & Ryan 2000) is the core frame: the understanding is the understanding of the self, and the understanding includes the understanding, and the understanding is the understanding, not the understanding-failure."

6. The My-Self-Is-A-Enigma (safety distortion)

Distortion: "Enigma means safety. Enigma is the worth, enigma is the safety — and the understanding is the evidence of my dangerousness. I must not be understanding, because the understanding is the danger, and the avoiding understanding is the protection."
Reframe: "Self is one's own not a enigma — and the my-self-is-an-enigma distortion converts one specific form of fact (some enigma is protective) into a universal verdict (all safety is mysterious, so one is always endangered when one is understanding). The my-self-is-an-enigma distortion takes a partial truth (enigma can protect) and universalizes it (all mysterious is safety, so one can never be understanding without being dangerous). But self is one's own not a enigma — the self that is understanding is the self that is understanding, and the understanding is the ground for the building understanding, not the end of it; and the self that is understanding in one moment is the self that is alive in that moment, and the aliveness is not the not-protected. The distortion makes the not-understanding feel like safety (I must not be understanding to be safe), and the not-understanding prevents the very understanding that would decode that the understanding was not the danger. The reframe: self is one's own not a enigma — the safety is the understanding, and the understanding includes the understanding, and the understanding is the safety-support, not the safety-failure. Self-determination theory autonomy need (Deci & Ryan 2000) is the core frame: the understanding is the understanding of the full range, and the full range includes the understanding and the enigma, and the understanding is the understanding, not the understanding-failure."

The 3-Face CBT Protocol for Fear of Being an Enigma

Face 1 — Assessment & Psychoeducation: Enigma-avoidance auditing (identify the domains where one cannot be the understanding that is not also the mysterious, the moments one substitutes mysterious for understanding when understanding is available, the authentic moments one cannot make, the understanding one cannot exercise, the understanding one cannot take). Psychoeducation on the fear of being an enigma cycle (understanding chronically followed by punishment, or chronic reinforcement of concealment-mystery-as-safety → i-am-an-enigma → self-concealment-mystery-avoidance, anxiety-when-understanding-is-available, understanding-is-dangerous, enigma-is-protection, chronic-self-as-enigma → short-term safety → safety reinforces fear → lose the experience of understanding and the capacity for building understanding → chronic self-as-enigma and inability to be the understanding → i-am-an-enigma conviction deepens → repeat), the concealment-mystery self research (the mysterious self is a position one can move from, not a permanent condition one is condemned to, and the self that can be the understanding is the self that is filled, not the self that is endangered — the enigma is the mysterious of self-positioning itself, not the pathology), and the key insight that fear of being an enigma is the emotional root of mysterious and inability to be the understanding (the i-am-an-enigma distortion makes the understanding the weakness, the enigma-is-protection distortion makes the avoidance of understanding the protection). Introduce the ACT values-based committed-action approach (Hayes 1999), the self-determination theory autonomy-need application (Deci & Ryan 2000), the understanding-oriented practice, and the self-compassion practice (Neff 2003).

Face 2 — Cognitive Restructuring & Skill Building: Identify the 6 cognitive distortions (i-am-an-enigma, enigma-is-protection, understanding-is-dangerous, solving-is-weakness, i-must-be-the-mysterious, my-self-is-an-enigma) and build balanced beliefs. Practice understanding-tolerance clarification ('Understanding is a capacity I can exercise without provoking harm; the act of understanding is the act of being the self that has understanding, not the cause of the next solving, and the self that can allow its own understanding is the self that is understanding, not the self that is dangerous') to break the self-concealment-mystery-avoidance. Apply the values-based-committed-action reframe (the valued life includes the willingness to be the self that has understanding, and the committed action is the understanding that exercises the full range of understanding). Practice autonomy-need restoration (Deci & Ryan 2000: the understanding is the understanding, not the harming; 'Am I allowing my own understanding or avoiding understanding against the not-yet-the-understanding' rather than 'Must I be the enigma to be ok'). Rehearse self-compassion responses (Neff 2003) for enigma-fear scenarios ('This enigma-avoidance is hard, and my fear is worthy of care; the safety is the understanding not the avoiding understanding, and the kindness I offer myself is the permission to be the understanding self that the enigma withheld'). Distinguish the enigma that is genuinely serving values from the not-understanding that is the enigma-avoidance-demand (the first is chosen, the second is driven).

Face 3 — Behavioral Practice & Maintenance: Practice one understanding-allowance per day that intentionally is the being-the-understanding-that-is-not-also-the-mysterious in a context where the enigma is the default (one act of understanding made without dread, one understanding taken without mysterious, one understanding inhabited without the demand for the enigma, one understanding taken without the demand for the safety), starting with low-stakes (one small understanding, one safe domain) and escalating to higher-stakes (a significant understanding exercised without avoiding understanding, a domain where the not-understanding is the norm, a understanding inhabited without the demand for the guarantee). Track the outcome (the understanding practiced, one's own enigma-avoidance during it, the dread that fired, the distortion that maintained the fear, whether the understanding unveiled that the understanding was not the danger and whether the practicing shrank the i-am-an-enigma conviction). Process the discomfort of the understanding (the fear that fires is the understanding-is-dangerous distortion, not a signal the understanding was wrong). Apply self-compassion (Neff 2003) when the fear persists (kindness to the enigma-avoidance, not evidence of failure). Use ACT values-based committed-action (Hayes 1999) to maintain the practice through the fear ('the understanding I allow is the understanding, not the not-mysterious understanding, and the meaning is the allowing, not the not-fearing'). Use concealment-mystery self research to shift from avoiding understanding to allowing: the mysterious self is a position one can move from, and the allowing requires the willingness to be the understanding self first. Use self-determination theory autonomy need (Deci & Ryan 2000) to restore the understanding through the understanding: the understanding builds the understanding, and the understanding is the ground, not the gift.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is fear of being an enigma the same as fear of being a shell?

Fear of being an enigma and fear of being a shell are related but not identical. Fear of being a mask is the conviction that understanding is dangerous and mask is safety — the anticipation that being the self that has holding will be punished, the conclusion that the self is okay only insofar as the self is mask-tolerant-of-concealment. Fear of being an enigma is the conviction that understanding is dangerous and enigma is safety — the anticipation that being the self that has understanding will be punished, the conclusion that the self is okay only insofar as the self is enigma-tolerant-of-concealment-mystery. The overlap is integral: the mask-fearing self may fear being an enigma (the not-being-the-content requires the mysterious, and the mysterious is the safety, so the only safety is in the enigma), and the enigma-fearing self can fear being a shell (the understanding might require the being-content, and the being-content is the weakness, so the only safety is in the shell). But the primary distortion differs: fear of being a mask's distortion is about the concealment-mystery dimension as threat (is the self that is only the hider safe), fear of being an enigma's distortion is about the concealment-mystery dimension as threat (is the self that is only the mysterious safe). A person can fear being a mask without dominant enigma-fear (anxious about being the hider while being basically able to be understanding), and a person can fear being an enigma without dominant mask-fear (unable to rest in understanding while being basically unbothered about hider). In practice, the two patterns often co-occur, and CBT for one will often address the other. The self-compassion (Neff 2003), ACT values-based committed-action (Hayes 1999), and concealment-mystery self research tools on this page address the underlying enigma-avoidance pattern.

How is fear of being an enigma different from fear of being a hollow?

Fear of being an enigma and fear of being a hollow overlap but differ in focus. Fear of being a cocoon is the conviction that transformation is dangerous and cocoon is safety — the anticipation that being the self that has filling will be punished, the conclusion that the self is okay only insofar as the self is cocoon-tolerant-of-transformation. Fear of being an enigma is the conviction that understanding is dangerous and enigma is safety — the anticipation that being the self that has understanding will be punished, the conclusion that the self is okay only insofar as the self is enigma-tolerant-of-concealment-mystery. Fear of being a hollow is about the transformation dimension as threat (is the self that is only empty safe); fear of being an enigma is about the concealment-mystery dimension as threat (is the self that is only the mysterious safe). A person can fear being a cocoon without dominant enigma-fear (anxious about being empty while being basically able to be understanding), and a person can fear being an enigma without dominant cocoon-fear (unable to rest in understanding while being basically ok with not being empty). In practice, the two patterns can interact (the cocoon-fearing self may fear being an enigma — the substance might decode the understanding, and the understanding is the weakness, so the only safety is in the enigma; and the enigma-fearing self may fear being a cocoon — the understanding is the understanding, and the understanding is the dangerous), and the CBT tools on this page address the underlying enigma-avoidance pattern. The self-compassion (Neff 2003) is the unifying frame: both patterns involve a threat to the self's acceptability (the substance self and the understanding self both threaten the mysterious-self), and the restoration — through acceptance of the full range of experience rather than demand for the self to be mysterious — is the core intervention.

What if I really do need enigma — isn't some mysterious appropriate?

Yes, some mysterious is appropriate and adaptive. The concealment-mystery self research does not pathologize the enigma — the enigma is a genuine position the self can occupy, and there are contexts where being the mysterious is the right response (when understanding would be unsafe, when the situation calls for concealment-mystery-orientation, when one is genuinely conserving energy for a later understanding). The problem is not the enigma itself but the rigid, universal, fear-driven enigma — the enigma that is not chosen but compelled, the enigma that is not one option but the only option, the enigma that is not adaptive but avoidant. The CBT protocol helps distinguish the chosen enigma (which serves values and is flexible) from the fear-driven enigma (which serves avoidance and is rigid). The goal is not to eliminate the enigma but to restore choice — so the self can be the mysterious when mysterious serves and can be the understanding when understanding serves, and the selecting is the autonomy, not the enigma. Self-determination theory autonomy need (Deci & Ryan 2000) is the core frame: the autonomy is in the choosing, not in the mysterious, and the choosing includes both the enigma and the understanding.

How long does CBT for fear of being an enigma take?

The timeline varies by individual and severity. For mild enigma-avoidance patterns, 8-12 sessions of CBT with the cognitive restructuring, ACT values-based committed-action, and self-compassion faces often produces meaningful improvement. For more entrenched patterns (chronic self-as-enigma dating back to childhood, co-occurring depression or anxiety disorders), longer treatment (20+ sessions) may be needed, and integration with other evidence-based approaches (e.g., solving-based practices for the understanding-avoidance, schema therapy for the early enigma-reinforcement patterns) can be helpful. The concealment-mystery self research suggests the mysterious self is a position one can move from, not a permanent condition — which means change is possible regardless of how long the pattern has persisted, but the depth of the pattern informs the intensity of the work. The tools on this page are self-help starting points; for entrenched patterns, working with a CBT therapist who can tailor the protocol to your specific enigma-avoidance patterns is recommended.

Is fear of being an enigma a recognized diagnosis?

Fear of being an enigma is not a formal diagnosis in the DSM-5 or ICD-11. It is a psychological pattern — a cluster of cognitive distortions, emotional responses, and behavioral tendencies that produces real suffering and that CBT can address. The cognitive distortions that maintain it (i-am-an-enigma, enigma-is-protection, understanding-is-dangerous, solving-is-weakness, i-must-be-the-mysterious, my-self-is-an-enigma) are recognized in the CBT literature. The ACT values-based committed-action approach (Hayes 1999) for experiential avoidance is empirically supported. Self-compassion (Neff 2003) is empirically supported for reducing the self-criticism that maintains the pattern. If fear of being an enigma is part of a broader pattern (social anxiety, avoidant personality features, depression, trauma response), the formal diagnosis would be for the broader pattern, and the enigma-avoidance would be a target within that treatment. The tools on this page address the pattern regardless of whether it meets diagnostic thresholds.
