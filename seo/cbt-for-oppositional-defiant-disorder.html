😤 CBT for ODD

Free Tools for Oppositional Defiant Disorder — For Parents and Children

Defiance Anger Argumentativeness Vindictiveness Parent Management Training
⚠️ Important: These tools are for educational purposes only. ODD is a clinical diagnosis that requires professional evaluation. If your child's behavior is causing significant impairment at home, school, or with peers, please consult a licensed child psychologist or behavioral therapist.
👨‍👩‍👧 For Parents: ODD treatment is most effective when it involves both parent management training (PMT) and individual CBT for the child. These tools support both tracks.

🎯 Defiance Patterns

  • Identify defiance triggers
  • Map antecedent → behavior → consequence
  • Recognize patterns of refusal
  • Track frequency and severity

🔥 Anger & Irritability

  • Anger thought records
  • Identify cognitive distortions
  • De-escalation techniques
  • Emotion regulation skills

💬 Argumentativeness

  • Analyze argument patterns
  • Identify "win at all costs" thinking
  • Practice active listening
  • Build collaborative problem-solving

😤 Vindictiveness

  • Identify revenge triggers
  • Process perceived injustices
  • Develop forgiveness skills
  • Build empathy perspective-taking
