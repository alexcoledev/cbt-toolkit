Common Cognitive Distortions for Immigrants

Immigration creates specific thinking patterns that CBT can address:

  • Outsider Distortion: "I don't belong here" — treating cultural difference as permanent exclusion
  • Language Anxiety: "My accent means I'm stupid" — conflating language ability with intelligence
  • Homesickness Grief: "I should have stayed" — romanticizing the past while discounting present gains
  • Discrimination Sensitivity: "They're all against me" — hypervigilance to microaggressions
  • Identity Conflict: "I've lost who I am" — seeing bicultural identity as loss rather than expansion
  • Assimilation Perfectionism: "I must be perfect to be accepted" — overcompensation for foreignness
  • Generational Gap: "My children are strangers" — parent-child cultural drift
