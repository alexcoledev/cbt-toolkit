📚 Common Athlete CBT Patterns

  • Catastrophizing: "If I lose this game, my career is over" → Challenge with evidence
  • All-or-nothing thinking: "I'm either the best or a failure" → Find the middle ground
  • Mind reading: "Everyone thinks I'm choking" → Focus on what you can control
  • Should statements: "I should never make mistakes" → Replace with preferences
  • Emotional reasoning: "I feel nervous, so I'll fail" → Feelings aren't facts
